# Fact sheet: Cyclone Arnab (landfall 28 Oct 2026)

These facts were checked against wire and Indian media reporting on 2 Nov 2026. Where
sources disagreed, the script uses the more conservative wording.

| Claim used in the Short | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Formed over the Bay of Bengal and strengthened before landfall | Confirmed | IMD: landfall early Wed 28 Oct as a *very severe* cyclonic storm, weakening to a *severe* cyclonic storm inland |
| Landfall near Narsapur, close to Bhimavaram, Andhra Pradesh | Confirmed | Reported as between Narsapur and Yanam. Bhimavaram is about 20 km inland from Narsapur |
| Sustained winds 125–135 km/h, gusts near 150 km/h | Confirmed | Reports range from 135 km/h to gusts of 140–150. The Short says "gusts near 150", not "over 150" |
| More than 100,000 residents evacuated (AP + southern Odisha) | Confirmed, but figures vary | Reports range from 55,000 (NDRF, early) to 89,000 to 180,000+ (AP official statement). Odisha planned about 48,000 |
| NDRF teams deployed | Confirmed | Team counts vary: 15 in AP, 31 requested, 39 total. The Short gives no team number |
| About 1,100 medical teams | Confirmed as "nearly 1,100" | The brief said "1,100+". The Short says "nearly 1,100" / "~1,100" |
| 30,000+ fishing boats secured | Confirmed | Officials: all 30,043 boats returned to shore |
| At least 3 deaths | Confirmed, but figures vary | Reports range from 3 to 7. The Short says "at least three" |
| Flash floods, inundated paddy fields, power and telecom damage | Consistent with reports | Konaseema was worst hit. About 79,000 acres of crops were damaged |
| Storm moving inland toward Chhattisgarh | **Not independently confirmed** | Taken from the editorial brief. Re-check before publishing |

The radar track in Scene 1 is **illustrative**: the intensity classes follow the IMD scale
(D → DD → CS → SCS → VSCS), but the plotted positions are not official best-track data.
The Scene 2 map is schematic, and its state boundaries are approximate. Both are labelled on screen.
